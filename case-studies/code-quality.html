Code quality and test coverage

Unit tests with JUnit 5 and Mockito that raise coverage measured by JaCoCo, SonarQube clean-up, and AI-assisted development with a company-hosted OpenCode setup.

Context

Banking services live for a long time and change often. Low test coverage and open static-analysis findings make every change riskier and slow down reviews.

Unit tests: JUnit 5, Mockito→Coverage: JaCoCo→SonarQube findings resolved→Code review in the Scrum team

My role

Alongside feature work, I took on improving the test coverage and SonarQube results of the services I worked on.

What I did

  • Wrote unit tests with JUnit 5, using Mockito to isolate services from their dependencies.
  • Measured coverage with JaCoCo and targeted untested paths to raise it.
  • Resolved SonarQube findings to improve maintainability.
  • Reviewed teammates' code as part of the Scrum team.
  • Used a company-hosted OpenCode setup to speed up coding, test writing and debugging, reviewing every suggestion before it went in.

Outcome

Higher measured coverage and fewer open SonarQube findings on the services I touched, giving the team more confidence when shipping changes.
